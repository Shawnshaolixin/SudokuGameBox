2. Third-Party SDKs

The App integrates the following third-party services, which may process device information such as the Advertising ID:

  • Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): serve rewarded and interstitial ads; UMP obtains user consent where required (e.g. EEA). See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Unity IAP): processes the "Remove Ads" non-consumable purchase and returns only purchase receipts; no personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: anonymous usage events and crash reporting.

3. Children's Privacy

The App is a casual game designed for all ages. It has no social features, chat, or user-generated content. Ads are served by third-party platforms in compliance with Google's ads and families policies; where legally required, "designed for families" settings will be applied.

4. Your Rights and Choices

  • Consent: in applicable regions (e.g. the EEA) you will see a UMP consent dialog on first launch and can choose to accept or reject personalized ads. You can manage this at any time in your device settings.
  • Deletion: the App stores no data on servers; all saves are local. Uninstall the App or clear its data in system settings to delete everything.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Purchases: buying "Remove Ads" disables ads for all time; purchases can be restored on the in-app store screen.
